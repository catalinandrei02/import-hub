## 3. Taxes in Canada

- **Dealer sale:** GST 5% + QST 9.975% normally. For the Q7, AED declared **"Raison exemption TVQ: EXPORTATION"** on the **ATAC form (SAAQ 6050, dealer-only)** and charged **GST only (999.95 CAD)**.
- **Zero-rated export:** a sale is zero-rated (no GST, no QST) when the **seller hands the vehicle to a carrier hired on behalf of the buyer** to ship it outside Canada. Source: Revenu Québec, *Exports and shipping of property*:
  https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/businesses/consumption-taxes/gsthst-and-qst/special-cases-gsthst-and-qst/exports-outside-canada-and-shipping-outside-quebec/exports-and-shipping-of-property/
  → This is why the dealer must **drop the car at Viamar / GO Transport itself**, not hand it to the uncle.
- **Private sale:** QST on a used vehicle bought from an individual is collected **by the SAAQ only when the registration is transferred**. No transfer (car exported) → no QST. https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/citizens/consumption-taxes/gst-and-qst/transport-and-road-vehicles/road-vehicles/purchase-of-a-road-vehicle/purchase-of-a-used-road-vehicle-from-an-individual/

## 4. Buying from a private seller (legal procedure)

1. **RDPRM** search by VIN (rdprm.gouv.qc.ca, a few dollars) → no lien / loan on the car. Viamar requires no lease or loan.
2. **Inspection** at an independent garage, with a VCDS/OBDeleven scan (generic OBD misses things; check readiness monitors — cleared codes are a red flag).
3. **Written contract** (any clear format is fine; printed and signed by both): names, addresses, phones, ID numbers, VIN, km, real price, date, "sold as is", and a clause that the seller hands the car to the buyer's carrier for export. A card payment alone is not enough — customs in NL and RAR need a bill of sale.
4. **Bank transfer** from the account of the buyer named in the contract (proof of payment for Dutch customs).
5. Seller **drops the car at GO Transport** (or Viamar picks it up, ~185 USD within Montreal).
6. After departure, seller **cancels the registration** at the SAAQ with the form for a vehicle sold outside Québec — **not "remisage"**:
   - https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/immatriculation/annuler-immatriculation-vehicule-emigre
   - Form 4815 (FR) / 4815A (EN): https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/blob/saaq/documents/formulaires/cancellation-registration-vehicule-licensed-sold-outside-quebec.pdf
- There is no ATAC equivalent between private persons.
- **Marlog** also offers purchasing assistance, escrow and pickup from private sellers in Canada (an option if a private deal feels risky).

## 11. Lessons learned

1. Budget Marlog at 1,030 €, not the 735 € quote.
2. Viamar adds surcharges after sailing; insurance is separate.
3. Some dealers refuse export; explaining "private buyer, family car, you just drop it at our carrier" changed Honda's answer.
4. The dealer, not the uncle, must deliver to the carrier — that is what makes the sale zero-rated.
5. Avoid plug-in hybrids (shipping restrictions and surcharges).
6. Always decode the trim from the VIN ("Premium Plus" = Progressiv).
7. Generic OBD scans miss things; ask for VCDS/OBDeleven.
8. Pay from the buyer's own account; customs use the contract value.
9. Canadian Audi warranty doesn't follow the car to Romania.
10. Private seller: no QST; seller cancels the registration as "émigré", not remisage.
